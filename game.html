What awaits you
Life on the Farm
🍇
Harvest & Brew
Grow grapes and apples, press them into juice — or into something a little stronger. Your recipe, your risk.
🚛
Choose Your Route
Every day, the road has opinions about your cargo. Pick your path to Redwater City wisely — and don't forget to make Fuel.
🎴
Card Events
Daily events twist the rules. A Mafia escort? A tavern stop? The cards decide what kind of day it's going to be.
🎣
Fishing
A quiet lake, a still rod, a reaction-based mini-game. A side hustle that doesn't appear on any police checklist.
👴
Grandpa's Wisdom
A grumpy old-timer who walks you through day one — then drops hints for the rest of the run. Ask too often and he falls asleep.
📊
Outsell Your Rival
Five days. One rival. Whoever moves the most product wins Redwater City. The Corleone Family isn't known for losing gracefully.
